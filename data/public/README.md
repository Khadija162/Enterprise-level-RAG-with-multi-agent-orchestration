# Open data provenance

The checked-in text in `open_knowledge.txt` is original, deliberately public educational material and is dedicated to CC0 1.0. It is not proprietary, personal, or private.

For a larger openly available corpus, consider the [NASA Open Data Portal](https://data.nasa.gov/) or public open government documents, but verify individual dataset terms before ingesting. This prototype does not automatically crawl third-party websites or redistribute their documents.

A production indexing job should track document license, URL, retrieval time, checksum, revision and removal requests.
